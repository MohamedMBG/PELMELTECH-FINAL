## What Closes Deals

✅ **ROI in months, not years.** Lead with numbers.  
✅ **Confidence on price.** No haggling = you're serious.  
✅ **Simple messaging.** Avoid tech jargon.  
✅ **Speed.** 8 weeks vs 6 months = huge advantage.  
✅ **Lock-in.** Recurring revenue = client can't leave.  

---

## What Kills Deals

❌ Price haggling. You lose every time.  
❌ "Let me think about it" → You never hear back.  
❌ No decision-maker on call.  
❌ "But our nephew codes..." (wrong fit).  
❌ Over-explaining tech. Keep it simple.  

---

## Red Flags to Skip

If prospect says any of these, politely decline:

- "Can you do it for 30K?" (serious negotiators go once to 40K max, then leave)
- "We want a website, nothing fancy" (wrong fit for 50K)
- "We'll negotiate payment terms" (cash flow problem)
- "No, we have someone internal" (lying, but not your problem)
- "Let's discuss next quarter" (not serious)

**Rule:** If you're spending >30 min negotiating price, walk away. There are 1000 other imprimeries.
